The Diagnosis

The "Variance" Trap

Most companies have an "average" culture score that hides deep pockets of toxicity.

We found a massive gap between the best and worst managers. 20% of your managers could be driving 80% of your attrition.

Step 01

Identify: Stop Guessing

You can't fix what you don't track. Generic engagement surveys often hide bad managers behind high-performing teams.

The Fix: Move from annual surveys to specific Manager Barometer surveys.

  • 1
    Isolate Scores
    Filter eNPS by Team Lead, not just Department.
  • 2
    Protect Anonymity
    Aggregate small teams (< 5) to ensure safety.
  • 3
    Spot the Trap
    Identify "Technical Experts" who fail as leaders.

Shift the manager's KPI from "Output" to "People Success."
